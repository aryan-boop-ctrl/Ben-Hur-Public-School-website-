OUR STORY

Growing Through Education

Ben-Hur Public School, Pilibhit, was established in 1993. The school's stated focus is academic excellence, moral values and the all-round development of students.

Its learning environment aims to help students build knowledge, confidence, responsibility and respect for cultural roots.

WHAT GUIDES US

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To nurture capable, confident and responsible learners prepared to contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To support learning through academic development, values, co-curricular opportunities and a caring school environment.
